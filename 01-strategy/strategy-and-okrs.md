# Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Become the essential platform connecting field teams and enterprise project management, making daily site reporting effortless while giving construction leaders real-time visibility and control.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Focus on existing enterprise construction customers, specifically field superintendents and foremen, by improving daily site reporting, progress tracking, and communication between field teams and project managers.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Win by delivering a mobile-first, intuitive field experience that makes daily reporting as simple as taking a photo or sending a message, while seamlessly connecting field updates to enterprise project management.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Excel at mobile-first UX, field workflow design, and seamless enterprise data integration. Build core field experiences, buy supporting infrastructure, and partner with construction customers for continuous validation.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Track weekly field adoption, reporting completion, and user satisfaction. Run monthly customer feedback reviews and quarterly strategy reviews to validate progress and adjust priorities.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not prioritize new advanced enterprise features until we improve field adoption. Adding more complexity would distract us from our core strategy: making Meridian the easiest way for field teams to report and collaborate.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Meridian the preferred daily reporting platform for field teams across existing enterprise customers.
- **KR1:** Increase weekly active field users from 20% to 60% by the end of Q1.
- **KR2:** Increase the percentage of daily field reports submitted through Meridian from 15% to 50% by the end of Q1.
- **KR3:** Increase 30-day retention among activated field users from 40% to 70% by the end of Q1.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The most valid challenge is our assumption that simplicity is the main adoption barrier. We have not yet validated whether the problem is usability, connectivity, or organizational resistance. Investing in UX improvements without understanding the root cause could fail to increase adoption.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I would validate the root causes of low adoption through field research and pilot testing before committing to major UX investments. I would defend our focus on existing enterprise customers and simpler field workflows while preserving the advanced capabilities enterprise buyers need.
